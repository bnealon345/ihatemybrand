Your homepage says "Quality service since 1987." So does every other shop in town.

Why it's costing you

A stranger can't tell what you fix, where you are, or why they'd pick you over the shop down the road. Your phone number is at the bottom of the page. People with a dead car don't scroll.

The fix

Lead with the job and the town: "Brakes, tires and engine work in Springfield. Most repairs done same day." Then put the phone number top right, big enough to tap with a thumb.
